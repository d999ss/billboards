# Domain Acquisition Map — Billboard / OOH SEO Network

Live whois-verified 2026-05-18. Raw results in `data/`.

## TLD strategy
**.com only.** OOH buyers (media planners, SMBs, agencies) are trust-sensitive and phone/quote-driven. `.io/.ai/.co` lower legitimacy and add zero SEO value here. All head-term alt-TLDs (`billboards.co/.net/.io/.ai`) are taken anyway. `.net` for the geo set is optional defensive only.

## Tier 1 — Flagship (authority entity, conversion hub)

| Domain | Status | Real query it owns | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BillboardPricing.com** | ✅ AVAILABLE | "billboard pricing", "billboard advertising pricing" | **Primary flagship** — highest-intent advertiser head query |
| **CostOfABillboard.com** | ✅ AVAILABLE | "how much does a billboard cost" | Top-of-funnel content magnet → 301/link into flagship |
| ListABillboard.com | ✅ AVAILABLE | supply-side (owners listing) | Hold — only if two-sided marketplace |
| BillboardPriceTag.com | ✅ AVAILABLE | weak/brandy | Skip |

Dropped: `BillboardQuote.com` — a CTA, not a search term. All brokerage/agency/marketplace brandables (`billboardbrokers`, `billboardmedia`, `billboardhub`, `billboardco`, `billboards.com`, `outdooradvertising.com`, etc.) already taken — an incumbent rolled up the brand layer. The open lane is the **geo-hyphen set**.

## Tier 2 — Geo expansion (the traffic engine)

| Pattern | SEO | Brand | Memorability | Expansion | Availability | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `<state>-billboards.com` (hyphen suffix) | 9 | 6 | 7 | 10 | **49/50 OPEN** | ✅ Acquire the set |
| `billboards<state>.com` (prefix) | 8 | 6 | 6 | 8 | 43/50 | Defensive only |
| `<state>billboards.com` (no hyphen) | 9 | 7 | 8 | 10 | 2/50 — incumbent owns | ❌ |
| `<State>Billboards.com` | 9 | 7 | 8 | 10 | 2/50 — same as above | ❌ |

No competitor owns a clean 50-state hyphenated set — this is the unused pattern.

### Acquisition list — 50-state coverage (~$700/yr)
49× `<state>-billboards.com` (all available) + `ny-billboards.com` (NY substitute — `new-york-billboards.com` is the only taken one in the pattern). NY fallbacks all available if preferred: `ny-billboards.com`, `newyork-billboards.net`, `new-york-billboards.net`, `nys-billboards.com`.

Not registrable: `.ut` / `.ny` are not TLDs (not in IANA root). Real geo-TLD option for NYC market only: `billboards.nyc` (unchecked).

## Tier 3 — Programmatic (URL paths, not domains)

```
BillboardPricing.com/                         national hub
  /[state]/                                   state pillar
  /[state]/[city]/                            city money page (P0)
  /[state]/[city]/[county|neighborhood]/      DMA refinement
  /[state]/[city]/digital-billboards/         format intent (P1)
  /[state]/[city]/billboards/[id]             inventory long-tail (P1, 1000s pages)
  /advertise/[industry]/                      advertiser intent — high CPC (P0)
  /near-me/                                   geo-IP dynamic
```
Geo domains canonical/301 into matching `/[state]/` path — capture exact-match SERP CTR, consolidate link equity into one rising entity.

## Total acquisition (~$730/yr)
`BillboardPricing.com` + `CostOfABillboard.com` + 49 `<state>-billboards.com` + `ny-billboards.com`.

## Competitor gaps (unused patterns)
1. Unified entity over a clean 50-state geo set (incumbents own fragmented microsites — can't consolidate).
2. Advertiser-intent pages: "billboard advertising for [industry] in [city]" — high-CPC, unguarded.
3. Per-structure inventory-ID pages — thousands-of-pages indexable surface incumbents can't scale.
